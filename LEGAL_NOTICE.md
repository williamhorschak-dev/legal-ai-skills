## Public support channels; no confidential intake

GitHub issues, pull requests, and any project discussions are public technical
collaboration channels, not channels for confidential legal intake or case review.
Do not submit real case files, identifying details, privileged material, financial
records, credentials, or other sensitive information. Use fictional examples for
bug reports. Do not assume communications with the maintainer are confidential or
protected by attorney-client privilege.

Support is limited to software use, reproducible defects, general documentation
corrections, and feature suggestions. The maintainer does not select claims or
remedies, calculate a person's legal deadlines, review their proposed filings, or
advise them whether to file, settle, appeal, or take another legal step. Requests
for that assistance should go to qualified counsel, not this project's issue queue.

Before providing information to an LLM host, consider that provider's terms,
privacy settings, and the permissions needed for the information. Installing a
skill does not establish confidentiality or privilege with an LLM provider.
